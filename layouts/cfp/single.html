Submission guidelines

What to send.

  • Abstract: No more than 250 words, clearly outlining the presentation and its connection to the DPRK.
  • Originality: Presentations must be original and not previously published or under consideration elsewhere.
  • Classification: Content must be TLP:CLEAR because the conference is open to the public.
  • Speaker information: Name, short bio, contact email, and Signal username.
  • Talk information: Title, public abstract, and a detailed outline shared only with reviewers.

Review process

How proposals are assessed.

Reviewers evaluate submissions for alignment with the conference theme, originality, technical accuracy, significance, and contribution to the field.

Organizers may invite shortlisted speakers to a brief one-to-one video call while submissions are under review.
