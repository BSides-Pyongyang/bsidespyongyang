The BSides Pyongyang Organizing Committee establishes the following Code of Conduct to preserve order, dignity, and mutual respect throughout the conference. All participants - attendees, speakers, sponsors, volunteers, supreme leaders and organizers are expected to uphold these standards without exception. The success of this event depends upon the collective discipline, worker harmony and integrity of everyone.

## 1. Upholding the Spirit of the Community

Participants shall conduct themselves in a manner befitting a serious technical gathering. Each individual is expected to demonstrate courtesy, composure, and responsibility at all times. Behavior that undermines the unity, focus, or professionalism of the event will not be tolerated. Simply put: conduct yourself honorably and treat others with respect.

## 2. Prohibition of Harassment and Intimidation

Harassment, intimidation, or any form of hostile behavior is strictly forbidden. This includes:

- Verbal or written abuse
- Unwelcome conduct of a personal or intrusive nature
- Threats, coercive behavior, or attempts to disrupt the peace of the event
- Targeting any participant on the basis of personal characteristics or viewpoints

These prohibitions apply universally across all roles and activities within the conference. Any action that creates discomfort, fear, or undue pressure upon others will be addressed with seriousness and urgency.

## 3. Standards of Expected Conduct

All participants shall:

- Maintain a respectful and cooperative attitude
- Observe personal and professional boundaries
- Resolve disagreements through calm and rational discussion
- Support an atmosphere conducive to learning, research, technical exchange and worker harmony

Each participant bears responsibility for contributing to a stable and orderly environment.

## 4. Reporting Procedures

Any participant who witnesses or experiences behavior contrary to this Code of Conduct is urged to report it promptly. All reports will be handled with discretion, seriousness, and respect for the individual submitting the information.

Concerns may be reported via email to: [bsidespyongyang+report@gmail.com](mailto:bsidespyongyang+report@gmail.com)

The Organizing Committee will investigate each matter thoroughly and take appropriate measures to safeguard the integrity of the event.

## 5. Commitment to Order and Respect

BSides Pyongyang is committed to providing a secure, respectful, and disciplined environment for all. By attending the conference, participants affirm their commitment to these principles and their willingness to uphold the conduct expected of them.
