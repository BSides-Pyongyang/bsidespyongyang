BSides Pyongyang returns for its second year, bringing cybersecurity researchers, engineers, and practitioners from around the world together for a focused technical program delivered online.

We invite original research and technical presentations covering cybersecurity, threat analysis, defensive security, incident response, and related fields. Submissions should be based on real-world experience, technical study, or applied research.

We are particularly interested in original research and case studies that advance the global understanding of cyber operations attributed to the Democratic People's Republic of Korea.
